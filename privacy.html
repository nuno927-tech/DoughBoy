DoughBoy

Privacy Policy

FLuX Studios · Effective September 29, 2026

The short version: DoughBoy does not collect your personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your recipes, bake log and photos stay on your phone. The only thing the app ever sends out on its own is a product's barcode number, when you scan one in the Pantry, to look up what it is.

Who we are

DoughBoy is a dough calculator for pizza, bread, flatbreads and sweet doughs for Android, made by FLuX Studios, an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It applies to the DoughBoy app (package com.fluxappstudios.doughboy) on Google Play.

What stays on your phone

Everything you create in DoughBoy is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. We never receive a copy. This includes:

  • Your recipes, favorites and their versions, saved styles and settings
  • Your bake log: ratings, notes and any photos you add
  • Pantry amounts and prices, planned bakes and your sourdough starters
  • Reminders, which are scheduled by Android on your phone
  • What the home-screen widget shows, which is kept on your phone for the widget

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android also keeps a copy of this data (not your bake photos) in your own Google account, so it comes back on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google, encrypted, and never shared with us. You can turn it off in your phone's Settings → Google → Backup.

What we collect

Nothing. DoughBoy has no servers, no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not sell or share any data.

Scanning barcodes in the Pantry

When you tap "Scan a barcode" in the Pantry, the camera reads the barcode on your phone. The camera image is processed on the device by Google's ML Kit and is never saved or uploaded.

DoughBoy then sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free, open database of food products, to look up the product's name and package size. Nothing else is sent: no account, name or other information about you. As with any internet request, Open Food Facts' servers see your device's IP address; their handling is covered by the Open Food Facts privacy policy. DoughBoy uses the answer to fill in the Pantry form and doesn't keep the barcode or send it anywhere else. Scanning is optional: you can always add pantry items by hand.

DoughBoy Pro purchases

DoughBoy Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. We never see your card or payment details. Google gives developers basic order records, such as the order number, date, country and amount, which we use only for purchase support and accounting. The app checks with Google Play whether you own Pro so it can unlock it and restore it on a new phone. The 7-day free trial is tracked only on your phone.

After a great bake, DoughBoy may show Google Play's own "rate this app" card. Google runs it and decides when it appears; we only see the public rating and review you choose to post.

Permissions the app uses

Notifications
To show reminders you set or turn on: making the final dough, feeding your starter, starting a planned bake, rating your dough and your bake, your weekly baking streak, and Bake mode timers. They can be turned off in Settings.
Alarms & reminders
So those reminders arrive on time, even when the phone is idle.
Camera and photos
Only when you choose to add a photo to a bake, or to scan a barcode in the Pantry. Bake photos are copied into the app's private storage and never uploaded. Barcode scans are read on the phone and the image isn't kept.
Internet
Only to look up a scanned barcode's product in Open Food Facts (see above) and to check Google Play for DoughBoy Pro.
Google Play billing
To buy and restore DoughBoy Pro.

When you share something

Some features send information out of the app, but only when you tap them and only to the place you pick in Android's share menu: recipe cards, recipe links and QR codes, spreadsheet and PDF exports of your bake log, and backup files. A shared recipe link contains only the recipe's name and dough settings. Once shared, the app or person you sent it to handles it under their own policies.

If you email us

"Contact the developer" opens your own email app with a message to us. If you send it, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply. We'll delete your messages if you ask.

Children

DoughBoy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You're in control. Delete individual favorites, bakes or plans in the app, or remove everything by clearing DoughBoy's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app. Backup and export files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

Security

Your data is kept in storage that Android protects from other apps. Using a screen lock on your phone helps keep it private.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy will say so before that version is released.
