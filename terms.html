Recipes, timings and safety

DoughBoy works out amounts, schedules, temperatures and bake times from common baking practice. They are a starting point: flour, ovens, kitchens and starters all vary, so results do too. Please use your own judgment:

  • Hot ovens, pizza ovens, steel, cast iron and boiling water can burn. Frying (doughnuts, churros, zeppole and others) uses very hot oil: never leave it unattended, keep children away, and keep a lid nearby.
  • Check ingredient labels for allergens such as wheat and gluten, eggs, milk, nuts and sesame. "Gluten-free" recipes are only as gluten-free as the ingredients you use.
  • Don't eat raw dough or batter made with raw flour or raw eggs.
  • Follow the food safety guidance where you live for keeping, chilling and storing dough, starters and baked goods.

DoughBoy isn't professional, medical or dietary advice. Tips from Dough trouble, the weather tip, troubleshooting and similar features are general suggestions.
